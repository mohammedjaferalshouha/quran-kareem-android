# Settings guide

## Display

- **App appearance:** choose light, dark, or system mode.
- **Background:** use the modern reading background.
- **Lock orientation:** prevent rotation while reading.
- **Landscape orientation:** use landscape when orientation lock is enabled.
- **Translated surah names:** show translated names where available.
- **Night reading:** use a dark reading page.
- **Text and background brightness:** adjust night-reading contrast.

## Reading

- **Page information:** show page, surah, and juz information.
- **Section markers:** notify at quarter and juz boundaries.
- **Bookmark highlighting:** mark saved ayahs.
- **Volume-key navigation:** move between pages with volume keys.

## Side-by-side pages

- **Two-page mode:** show consecutive pages in landscape.
- **Quran and translation:** show Quran text on the right and the selected translation on the left while preserving the current page and reading position.

## Tafsir and translation

- Download or remove tafsir and translation files.
- Show the ayah before its translation or tafsir.
- Select an accessibility-oriented font where available.
- Adjust Quran and translation text sizes independently.

## Audio and downloads

- Prefer streaming or local downloads.
- Select the download amount.
- Manage downloaded audio.
- Choose the reciter from the bottom reader bar.

## Reader shortcuts

- **Sun and moon:** switch appearance quickly.
- **Magnifier:** open search.
- **Plus and minus:** increase or decrease Quran text size.
- **Reading information:** show surah, ayah, page, juz, hizb, and quarter.
- **Overflow menu:** open tafsir, translation, and additional commands.
